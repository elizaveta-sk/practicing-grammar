# Transcription test sentences (Phase 1, task 1.6)

Say each sentence **exactly as written, mistakes included**. A good provider writes it down unchanged.
Paste the sentence into the "What will be said" box first. Better still: ask a Russian- and a Japanese-speaking student to record a few as voice messages and upload those.

## Missing articles (very common for both Russian and Japanese speakers)
1. If I won lottery, I would buy house.
2. I would like to be best at piano in world.
3. If I could live anywhere, I would choose small village near sea.

## Missing -s / -ed endings
4. He have lived here for three year.
5. She go to work every day.
6. I have work there since 2019.

## Wrong tense or verb form (the target grammar)
7. If I would have money, I will travel.
8. If I was invisible, I will go to the bank.
9. I have went to Japan last year.
10. If I had knew, I would came earlier.
11. If it will rain, I stay home.

## Contractions vs full forms (must not be swapped)
12. I'd buy a house.
13. I would buy a house.
14. I'd have gone if I'd known.

## Word order and missing words
15. If I could, I would to travel to the past.
16. I would like meet Einstein.
17. What you would do if you were rich?

## Correct sentences (should stay correct)
18. If I could have any job, I would be a pilot.
19. I've lived in Tokyo for five years.
20. If you heat ice, it melts.

## Pauses and fillers
21. If I... um... if I could travel in time, I would, uh, see the future.
